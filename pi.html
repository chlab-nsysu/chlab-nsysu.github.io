ABOUT THE PI

Chun-Han Chang, Ph.D.

Dr. Chun-Han Chang is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Marine Biotechnology and Resources at National Sun Yat-sen University, Taiwan. His research focuses on marine natural products, oxidative stress, healthy aging, neurodegeneration, and environmental toxicology.

His research integrates natural product bioactivity screening, molecular biology, and whole-organism experimental models, particularly Caenorhabditis elegans, to investigate biological effects and potential mechanisms of natural and environmental compounds.

Before joining NSYSU, he completed postdoctoral research at National Taiwan University and the National Cerebral and Cardiovascular Center in Japan, with projects covering metabolic and cardiovascular aging.

RESEARCH PROJECTS

Research experience

Marine natural products · NSYSU

Screening and mechanistic investigation of compounds derived from marine organisms and microorganisms for stress resistance, aging-associated phenotypes, and neuroprotection using C. elegans.

Cardiovascular aging · NCVC

Characterization of a spontaneous atrial fibrillation mouse model, cardiovascular remodeling and fibrosis, and investigations into senescent cell clearance.

Food bioactives · NTU

Natural compounds, metabolic regulation, circadian rhythm and gut microbiota-related research; bioactivity testing of essential oils and analytical characterization.

Environmental toxicology · NTU

Organic selenium and aging-related phenotypes in C. elegans; nanomaterial toxicity; heavy-metal and contaminated-sediment investigations.
