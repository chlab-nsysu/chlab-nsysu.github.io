OUR SCIENTIFIC FOCUS

Research

From marine-derived compounds to whole-organism biology: integrating natural product discovery with experimental models of stress, aging, and neurodegeneration.

RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

Exploring bioactive compounds through mechanistic biology.

Our laboratory studies the biological potential of natural products, with an emphasis on marine-derived compounds and their effects in experimental model systems.

We use Caenorhabditis elegans to connect molecular hypotheses with measurable phenotypes. The research directions below describe our areas of investigation; specific biological effects require experimental validation in each study.

Research direction 01

Marine Natural Products & Bioactive Compounds

Marine organisms and microorganisms produce structurally diverse natural products that provide opportunities to investigate biological activity. Our research explores marine-derived compounds as candidates for further biological characterization.

We combine compound-focused screening with organism-level observations to prioritize promising candidates and formulate testable mechanistic hypotheses.

Marine-derived metabolitesBioactivity screeningMechanistic hypotheses
Scientific questions
Which marine-derived compounds exhibit measurable biological activity at non-toxic concentrations? How reproducible are their effects across phenotypic assays?

Research direction 02

Oxidative Stress, Stress Resilience & Healthy Aging

We use Caenorhabditis elegans as a whole-organism platform to examine the relationships among environmental stress, redox balance, stress tolerance, and lifespan-associated phenotypes.

Depending on the study, experimental readouts may include survival after stress exposure, reactive oxygen species indicators, lifespan analysis, and fluorescent reporters for stress-responsive signaling pathways.

Oxidative stressStress toleranceLifespan phenotypesDAF-16 / SKN-1 signaling
Scientific questions
Do candidate compounds modify stress resistance or aging-associated phenotypes? Which signaling pathways merit subsequent validation?

Research direction 03

Neurodegeneration & Disease-Related Phenotypes

Neurodegenerative conditions involve complex changes in neuronal integrity, protein homeostasis, and cellular stress responses. Our experimental work incorporates C. elegans disease-related models to explore these processes.

Relevant readouts may include α-synuclein-associated aggregation phenotypes, dopaminergic neuron integrity, and behavior. These assays support mechanistic investigation but are not substitutes for clinical validation.

Parkinson’s disease-related modelsα-Synuclein phenotypesDopaminergic neuronsProteostasis
Scientific questions
How do candidate compounds affect disease-related phenotypes and neuronal stress? Can complementary assays distinguish general toxicity from potentially protective responses?

EXPERIMENTAL STRATEGY

From candidate to mechanism

A flexible experimental framework, adapted to each project and the evidence available.

STEP 01

Candidate selection

Source and prioritize natural compounds with clear identities and appropriate controls.

STEP 02

Phenotypic assessment

Establish tolerated exposure conditions and quantify relevant biological responses.

STEP 03

Mechanistic testing

Use complementary reporter, genetic, imaging, or biochemical approaches as appropriate.

STEP 04

Evidence integration

Assess reproducibility, limitations, and the need for additional model validation.

Specific methods and pathways vary by project. Published findings will be linked in the Publications section as the website is updated.
