從海洋活性成分
探索健康老化From marine bioactives to healthier aging.

我們以秀麗隱桿線蟲（Caenorhabditis elegans）為模式，篩選海洋天然物中的候選活性成分，研究它們如何影響生物體的壓力反應、老化與神經退化相關變化。

We use the nematode Caenorhabditis elegans to screen marine-derived compounds and study how they affect stress responses, aging, and processes associated with neurodegeneration.

系所簡介About the Department

海洋生物科技暨資源學系Department of Marine Biotechnology and Resources

國立中山大學海資系以海洋生命科學為基礎，結合生物科技與化學，研究海洋生物及其資源。系上設有學士、碩士及博士班，教學與研究涵蓋海洋生物科技、天然物研究，以及海洋生物資源的永續利用與保育。

The department at National Sun Yat-sen University combines marine life science, biotechnology, and chemistry to study marine organisms and their resources. Its undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programs cover marine biotechnology, natural products, and the sustainable use and conservation of marine bioresources.

你可以在海資系學到什麼？What can you study here?

  • 海洋科學與生命科學基礎Foundations in marine and life sciences
  • 海洋生物科技與實驗技能Marine biotechnology and laboratory skills
  • 海洋生物資源開發與生態保育Marine bioresource development and ecological conservation

實驗室定位About Chang Lab

用線蟲研究海洋天然物的生物作用Studying marine natural products in a living model

Chang Lab 由張鈞涵老師主持，位於國立中山大學海資系。我們用秀麗隱桿線蟲觀察候選化合物在完整生物體中的作用，再追問哪些基因與細胞路徑參與其中。

Led by Dr. Chun-Han Chang, Chang Lab uses C. elegans to observe how candidate compounds act in a whole organism and investigate which genes and cellular pathways contribute to those effects.

我們怎麼做研究How we study a candidate compound

  1. 從海洋天然物中篩選候選活性成分Screen marine natural products for candidate bioactives
  2. 測量線蟲的抗壓能力、壽命或神經相關表型Measure stress resistance, lifespan, or neuronal phenotypes in worms
  3. 用螢光報導系統或遺傳工具驗證作用機制Use fluorescent reporters or genetic tools to test how the compound acts

海洋天然物與活性篩選Marine Natural Products & Bioactivity Screening

哪些來自海洋生物或微生物的天然物，值得進一步研究？我們在線蟲中測試候選成分，評估它們是否產生可重複量測的生物反應。

Which natural products from marine organisms or microbes warrant further study? We test candidate compounds in worms for measurable, reproducible biological responses.

海洋天然物研究（英文）Marine natural products research ↗

氧化壓力與健康老化Oxidative Stress & Healthy Aging

候選成分能否改變線蟲面對氧化壓力或熱壓力時的反應？我們測量存活與壽命，並追蹤與抗壓和老化有關的訊息路徑。

Can candidate compounds change how worms respond to oxidative or heat stress? We measure survival and lifespan and investigate signaling pathways involved in stress responses and aging.

老化研究（英文）Aging research ↗

神經退化與環境暴露Neurodegeneration & Environmental Exposure

天然物與環境污染物如何影響神經退化相關表型？我們在線蟲的帕金森氏症相關模型中，觀察多巴胺神經元與 α-突觸核蛋白聚集的變化。

How do natural compounds and environmental pollutants affect neurodegeneration-related traits? In worm models of Parkinson's disease, we examine dopaminergic neuron integrity and changes in α-synuclein aggregation.
