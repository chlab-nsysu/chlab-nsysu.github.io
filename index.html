Who we are

Connecting marine resources with biomedical discovery.

Our laboratory is based in the Department of Marine Biotechnology and Resources at National Sun Yat-sen University, Taiwan.

We study naturally occurring bioactive compounds and their potential roles in oxidative stress responses, healthy aging, and disease-related phenotypes. The nematode C. elegans provides an accessible whole-organism platform for hypothesis-driven research.
